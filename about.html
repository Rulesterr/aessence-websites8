About

A quieter kind of care.

We make botanical essentials for people who'd rather own a few things they love than many they tolerate.

Aessence started in a small kitchen with a handful of ingredients and a stubborn belief that good products don't need to shout. We were tired of crowded labels, vague claims, and routines that felt like chores.

So we stripped everything back. We chose single-origin botanicals we could trace and trust. We blended in small batches so nothing sat on a shelf losing its character. And we put every ingredient, in plain language, right on the front.

Today that philosophy still guides every decision we make — from the farms we partner with to the recyclable packaging we ship in.
